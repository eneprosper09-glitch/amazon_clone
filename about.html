About Amazon Clone

A simple online shopping experience designed to make finding and buying products easier.

Who We Are

Amazon Clone is a student-built e-commerce website created to demonstrate how a modern online shopping platform works.

Our goal is to provide customers with an easy way to browse products, save favourites, add items to their cart and complete orders.

Our Mission

To create a simple, convenient and enjoyable shopping experience for every customer.

Our Vision

To build an accessible online store where customers can easily discover products they need.

Customer First

We focus on making navigation, shopping and customer support simple and straightforward.

Built With Technology

This project uses HTML, CSS and JavaScript to create an interactive shopping experience.

What You Can Do

Browse our products, search for items, add products to your cart, save products to your wishlist and manage your orders.
